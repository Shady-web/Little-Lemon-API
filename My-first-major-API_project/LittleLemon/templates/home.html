Our Story

A Family Tradition

Little Lemon was founded in 2018 by cousins Mario and Adriana, who grew up on their grandmother's recipes in a small village outside Athens. After years of working in kitchens across Europe, they brought those treasured flavours to Chicago.

Every dish on our menu is rooted in tradition — slow-cooked stews, wood-fired breads, and seafood sourced daily from local markets. We believe food should be an experience, not just a meal.

Our chefs combine age-old techniques with seasonal ingredients and a contemporary sensibility, making each visit to Little Lemon a little different and always memorable.
